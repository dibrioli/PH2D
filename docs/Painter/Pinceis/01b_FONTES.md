Traço descrito nas fontes [V]: Studio Pen liso, engrossa com a pressão mas varia pouco · Technical Pen largura
uniforme (Rapidograph) · Gesinski Ink pena/caneta-pincel translúcida que escurece na sobreposição · Ink Bleed borda
áspera que sangra · Syrup chapado sem estria · Monoline largura constante sem pressão · Shale macio e muito
estabilizado · Derwent lápis de cor que quase não responde à pressão · Peppermint mais limpo que o Soft Pastel ·
Bonobo Chalk aerógrafo granulado · Blackburn em blocos, alterna sólido/granulado · Nikko Rull textura de rolo ·
Turpentine a textura de óleo de referência · Lightpen fio que brilha · Noise Brush grão e sombra numa passada.

## §3 — Photoshop · Fresco · Clip Studio Paint

**Photoshop** — pastas General Brushes (Soft Round, Hard Round, Hard Round Pressure Opacity [V]; Soft Round Pressure
Size/Opacity [V]), Dry Media, Wet Media (Kyle's Paintbrush Wet Blender, Kyle's Impressionist Blender [V] — trocam a
ferramenta para Smudge), Special Effects (Kyle's Spatter, Concept, Screentones [M]). Packs do Kyle T. Webster [V]:
Megapack, Watercolor, Dry Media, Gouache, Spatter (40+), Runny/Manga Inkers, Crosshatchers, Rake, Impressionist,
Letterers, Halftones, Art Markers, Charcoal; Basic Inker. **Mixer Brush** [V]: Wet, Load (acaba no traço), Mix, Flow,
Sample All Layers, Load/Clean after each stroke. **Smudge** [M]: Strength, Finger Painting. **Brush Settings**: Tip
Shape (Size, Angle, Roundness, Hardness, Spacing, Flip); tip **Erodible** (Softness = velocidade do gasto, Sharpen
Tip [V]); tip **Bristle** (Bristles, Length, Thickness, Stiffness [V], Angle); tip Airbrush (Hardness, Distortion,
Granularity, Spatter Size/Amount); Shape Dynamics (jitters + Control: Fade, Pressure, Tilt, Wheel, Rotation, Initial
Direction, Direction); Scattering (Both Axes, Count, Count Jitter [V]); Texture (Texture Each Tip, Mode, Depth, Min
Depth, Depth Jitter, Invert [V]); Dual Brush (Mode, Size, Spacing, Scatter, Count — «quebra o contorno» [V]); Color
Dynamics (Apply Per Tip, FG/BG, H/S/B Jitter, Purity); Transfer (Opacity, Flow, Wetness, Mix Jitter); Brush Pose
[V]; flags Noise, Wet Edges, Build-up, Smoothing, Protect Texture; Smoothing (Pulled String, Catch-up) [V].

**Fresco** [V] — Live Watercolor: Round Detail, Wash Soft, Wash Flat, Wet Spatter; ajustes Flow e Water Flow; nunca
seca sozinha (Layer Options › Dry Layer). Live Oil: Flat, Round, Filbert, Detail; ajustes Flow, Paint Mix (0 = cor nova
pura; ~25 = a cor nova domina o início e morre ao apanhar a da tela), Reload color; opacidade 0 % = blender.

**Clip Studio Paint** [V] — Pen: G-pen (rígida), Real G-pen (borda áspera), Mapping pen (sem AA por omissão),
**Turnip pen** (a favorita: fino→grosso suave), Tapered pen (afina rápida), Calligraphy, Textured/Roughness pen.
Pencil: **Rough pencil** (o favorito), Real pencil, Darker pencil, Lighter pencil, Mechanical pencil, Colored pencil,
Design pencil (tilt), Tapered pencil; Pastel: Charcoal, Crayon, Pastel, Chalk. Watercolor: Opaque, Smooth, Wet wash,
Watercolor splash, Round/Flat, Soft bleed, Rough wash, Textured blender, Watery brush. Thick paint: Oil paint, Oil paint
flat, Round mixing, Thick oil paint, Gouache, Dry gouache, Thin gouache, Pointillism, Gouache blender, Color change.
India ink: Bit husky, Rough brush, Brush pen, Dry ink, Watery ink, Wet blotting ink. Decoration (Dappled light,
Glitter, Leaves, Grass, Cloud, Hatching…). Blend: Blur, Blend, Finger tip, Copy stamp, Textured blender. Eraser: Hard,
Soft, Kneaded, Rough, Snowy, Vector. Propriedades: Ink › **Color mixing** (Blend · **Running color** · Smear), Amount
of paint, Density of paint, **Color stretch**; Color jitter; Anti-aliasing; Spraying effect; Stroke (Ribbon); Texture
(Apply by each plot); 2-Brush shape; **Watercolor edge** (largura, Opacity, Darkness; 5.1+ Standard/Vivid);
Correction (Stabilization, Adjust by speed); Starting and ending.

## §4 — Corel Painter · ArtRage · Krita (motores)

**Corel Painter** [V] — categorias: RealBristle; Thick Paint (Paint Load, Brush Density); Watercolor (camada);
Digital Watercolor (franja instantânea); Real Watercolor; Real Wet Oil; Acrylics; Oils; Gouache; Pastels; Chalk &
Crayons; Charcoal & Conte; Pencils (largura pela velocidade); Pens; Markers (acumulam e poçam); Airbrushes; Blenders
(Just Add Water, Grainy Water); Artists (Sargent, Impressionist, Seurat); Impasto; Palette Knives; Pattern Pens;
Particles (Gravity/Flow/Spring); Dynamic Speckles; Sponges; F-X (Glow, Fire, Fairy Dust); Image Hose; Cloners;
Erasers (Bleach, Darkener); Glazing (opacidade sobe ao longo do traço); Liquid Ink; Sumi-e. Dab types: Circular,
Computed Circular, Single-Pixel, Static Bristle, Captured, Camel Hair (cor por pêlo), Flat (bearing+tilt), Palette
Knife, Bristle Spray, Airbrush, Pixel/Line Airbrush, Projected, Rendered, Liquid Ink, Watercolor, Artists' Oils (carga
que esgota), Thin Line Particles, Dynamic Speckle.

**ArtRage** [V] — Oil Brush (Thinners, Loading, Insta-Dry, Autoclean, Stiffness, Aspect, Square Head); Palette Knife
(Flat Blade, Edge Blade, Soft, Blur, Wet; Lock Rotation); Paint Tube; Paint Roller; Watercolor (Thinners, Loading,
Color Bleed, Paper Wet); Airbrush (não achata a tinta de baixo); Pencil (Softness 2H→6B, Precise, Tilt Angle, Noise);
Chalk; Wax Pastel; Felt Pen; Ink Pen (Smoothing, Tapering); Gloop Pen (a gota expande parada); Glitter Tube; Sticker
Spray. Lei comum: tudo reage à textura da tela e mistura com a tinta existente.

**Krita** [V/M] — 18 motores: Pixel (12 modos de textura, Sharpness, Lightness Strength p/ ponta RGBA) · Color Smudge
(Smear × Dulling, Smudge Length/Radius, Color Rate, Overlay, Lightness/Height + Paint Thickness) · Bristle (ink
depletion, soak, shear) · Chalk · Clone · Curve (teia de histórico) · Deform (liquify) · Dyna (massa e arrasto) ·
Filter · Grid · Hatching · MyPaint · Particle · Quick · Shape/Experiment · Sketch (Harmony) · Spray · Tangent Normal.
Mais votados no krita-artists [V]: Basic-5 Size/Opacity (4), Pencil-6 Quick Shade (3, «o mais parecido com grafite
real»), Charcoal Pencil Large (2), Basic-3 Flow, Charcoal Rock Soft, Blender Smear, RGBA Thickpaint (1 cada). Fluxo de
retrato citado: Basic-1 → Wet Paint → Wet Textured Soft → Wet Paint Details → Blender Basic → Airbrush Soft (Multiply).
