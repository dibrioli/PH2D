**Design 3 — 7.5/10.** Best risk articulation (the F-table), best hold (derived predicate, zero new state), the flag-hygiene gate, the pending↔preview invariant gate, and the strongest oracle (`one_shot == live_feed`, bit-equal grids). **Design 2 — 6/10.** Best product narrative ("the sketch melts" — adopted as the smoke script's language) and the right explicit relief law. Two structural flaws: (a) the `flush_direct_stroke` engine door + `Trail::flush_window` rest on a **refuted fact** ("an Anchored single-dab deposit vanishes" — false, verified above; its red-first gate #2 would be born GREEN); (b) the commit door re-tiles manually (`tiled_dabs_grouped` at the door) — a second tiling call-site that can drift from `stamp_dabs_routed`'s exact invocation, where replaying through `stamp_dabs` gets identity for free. ### Adjudicated disputes (do not re-litigate)
1. **No engine change in this wave.** The single-dab lane transfers (verified, above). The only real loss at `end_direct_stroke` (`crates/ph2d-wet-paint/src/painter/doors.rs:217-222` per Reader 3) is the ≤2-dab tail per multi-dab lane — **byte-parity with what every live pen-up already ships**. If the smoke shows a visible gap at a closed contour's seam, the named fix is a small engine flush door in `doors.rs` — a follow-up with its own gate, not this wave.
2. **The undo-path peel is NOT converted to the re-arming peel door** (`P/shape_snapshot.rs:71-79`). Re-arming there would keep the session alive across an undo restore — the guard's whole law is that undo kills the water. Comment this at the site.
3. **Authoring previews are UN-OWNED, not owned-with-keep-lerp.** Un-owning makes the selection wrapper (`P/stamp_route.rs:108-125`) apply to the preview byte-identically to Paint mode; the commit composite enforces the same gates at the single canvas write (`P/wetpaint.rs:593-610`). One law, zero new gate code.
4. **The eraser × re-stamp methods = flat erase; the water dies.** The flat EraseAlpha preview shows exactly what commits (preview honest); the session dies as a foreign write at the next guard — the same stance watercolor takes (Reader 4 §4) and the deliberate extension of W2.6. D2's engine-erase alternative was rejected: it makes the preview lie (erases base visually, erases pigment on commit).
5. **Line is a shape EDITOR** (routed via `route_shape_pointer_multi`, `P/canvas_pointer.rs:73-81`): its commit is Enter/Apply (`P/line_commit.rs:31-45`), never pen-up. The task statement's "mouse-up for Line" is corrected; DragDot/Anchored commit at pen-up.

## PART B — THE FINAL DESIGN (D1 skeleton + grafts)

### The four laws
- **Law A (authoring):** in WetPaint mode a non-incremental batch is NOT owned by the wet module — it falls through the completely normal flat pipeline (wrapper + colour routes), with the impasto height pass explicitly skipped (wet lays no relief; graft from D2). Non-wet modes: the predicate short-circuits on mode first — not one instruction changes.
- **Law B (recording):** `stamp_drag_preview` (`P/stamp_preview.rs:33-84`) is the ONE function that paints every re-stamp preview (stroke methods via `stamp_stroke_dabs` match at `P/stamp_preview.rs:222-229`; all shape editors + parked + boolean via `restamp_shapes_preview` → `P/stroke_multi.rs:235`). Its tail stashes the exact batch it just painted (`pending_deposit`) — the deposit is the preview BY CONSTRUCTION; nothing is re-derived at commit (seed/offset/boolean-trace drift structurally impossible).
- **Law C (one door):** `commit_drag_preview` (`P/stamp_preview.rs:116-123`) — already called by every commit path: pen-up (`P/stroke_lifecycle.rs:309`), `line_commit`/`line_commit_keep` (`P/line_commit.rs:39,59`), `curve_commit`/`curve_commit_keep` (`P/curve_commit.rs:17,37`), `commit_open_shape` (`P/curve_commit.rs:60`), the per-editor keeps, and the method-switch bake (`P/stroke_ctl.rs:25` → `commit_open_shape`). Its new wet branch peels the flat preview, re-arms the guard, and replays the stash ONCE through the full `stamp_dabs` dispatcher with `deposit_pass = true` — which is the third key on the wet arm's gate.
- **Law D (wet-on-wet):** the live session SURVIVES authoring. The preview's writes are OWNED writes (re-arm `sess.canvas = Arc::clone(&self.canvas_rgba)` — the composite's own pattern, verified `P/wetpaint.rs:613-614`); the sim HOLDS while a preview is live (derived predicate, graft from D3 — no new state, no release door); foreign swaps (undo, layer switch, fill) never re-arm and still die at the guard (`P/wetpaint.rs:518-524`), which keeps running every tick during authoring (`:485`). Consequence stated as product law: **the water freezes while you author** (flow AND drying) — forced by mechanism (any composite copies `sess.base` verbatim where pigment α=0, `P/wetpaint.rs:579-583` region, and would erase the preview inside its dirty rect: a torn state) and it IS the owner's "static preview / sim at commit". Commit ordering is load-bearing: **peel → re-arm → deposit** (without the re-arm the guard at `P/wetpaint.rs:190` kills the session and the deposit lazily creates a fresh one at `:197-213` — visually similar, nothing fuses: the silent theft of the differential).

### Session lifecycle (in words)
LIVE (water flowing; tick: guard → knobs → steps → composite; composite re-arms guard) → artist starts a re-stamp method / shape: first `stamp_drag_preview` restores nothing, saves pristine, stamps flat through the wrapper (Arc forks), records the stash, re-arms the guard; `drag_preview` is now Some ⇒ **AUTHORING-HOLD** (tick: guard only, zero steps, zero composites — water frozen; drying frozen). Every gesture/panel-change refill repeats the dance (restore → save → stamp → stash overwritten → re-arm). **COMMIT** (pen-up / Enter / Apply / Apply&Keep / method-switch bake) → `commit_drag_preview` wet branch: peel → re-arm → `deposit_pass` replay through `stamp_dabs` (lanes born/matched with real chords, per-dab Randomize ink, one composite over the SAME frozen `sess.base` — the deposit FUSES with the old water) → relief teardown → `wetpaint_stroke_end` ⇒ `drag_preview` None ⇒ hold releases ⇒ next tick the sim resumes: old + new pigment flow together ("the sketch melts"). **CANCEL** (Esc): peel door (re-arm) ⇒ session and water survive, hold releases, water resumes exactly as it was. **FOREIGN** (undo / layer switch mid-authoring): Arc swap without re-arm ⇒ guard kills the session at the next tick; authoring continues dry; commit deposits into a fresh session whose lazily-frozen `base` is the post-peel canvas (peel runs FIRST — the sketch is never in the base, so paint never exists twice). **Dry canvas**: no session during authoring; commit's deposit lazily creates one (`P/wetpaint.rs:197-213`) after the peel. **Mode-leave**: `wetpaint_end_session` = the bake (session None; stash cleared).
